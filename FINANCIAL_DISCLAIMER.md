Historical relationships do not guarantee that similar

## 4. Backtesting and Paper Trading

Backtesting and paper-trading results do not establish
that a strategy will perform similarly in live markets.

Simulations may differ from actual trading due to:

- Transaction costs.
- Bid/ask spreads.
- Slippage.
- Liquidity constraints.
- Market impact.
- Execution delays.
- Data quality and survivorship bias.
- Corporate actions and adjustment methodology.
- Regulatory and operational restrictions.

Simulated returns should not be interpreted as
realized investment returns.
