# Event Day 0? After-Hours Earnings Announcements (Berkman & Truong, 2009)

**Basis:** abstract only (EconPapers and a library catalogue record), opened 2026-11-08; full text paywalled. Scored Weak because the method could not be read.

**Takeaway:** More than 40% of US earnings announcements are made after hours, and their price and volume response appears one trading day after the Compustat or I/B/E/S date, so event day 0 must be shifted for after-hours releases.

## Problem
Do standard earnings-date fields mis-date the market reaction for after-hours announcements?
