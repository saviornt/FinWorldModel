# ACLED End User Licence Agreement (ACLED, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** ACLED's conflict-event data are licensed for non-commercial use (commercial entities need a corporate licence), may not be used as training data or embeddings for any model without written consent, and must be deleted on termination.

## Problem
What ACLED allows for storage, commercial use and model training.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Read the EULA (the Terms of Use page only links to it).

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- s. 1.2: commercial entities may not access or use the content without a corporate licence; s. 3.1 grant is non-commercial.
- s. 7.1: no use as "training data, embeddings, or knowledge-base input" without express prior written consent.
- s. 7.2: no automated access outside the API.
- s. 3.1: external outputs must be transformative and not allow reverse engineering of the dataset.
- s. 13.3(ii): delete all licensed content on termination.
