# Alpaca Terms and Conditions and redistribution FAQ (AlpacaDB, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** Alpaca's content (including market data) is for personal, non-commercial use, may not be copied or republished, and may not be redistributed without written consent; Pro plan users also accept the Nasdaq and NYSE subscriber agreements; model training is not mentioned.

## Problem
What Alpaca's terms allow for storing data and training models.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Read the public Terms and Conditions PDF (no section numbers, no date shown) and the support article on redistribution (November 2022).

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- "Content is provided exclusively for personal and noncommercial access and use."
- No part of the Content may be "copied, reproduced, republished"; no commercial transmission without prior written consent.
- Pro plan: users agree to the Nasdaq OMX Global Subscriber Agreement or NYSE display agreement (see [[Nasdaq 2026 Global Subscriber Agreement]]).
- ML/AI training: not mentioned. Storage: no clause.
- Support FAQ: "you cannot redistribute Alpaca API data."
