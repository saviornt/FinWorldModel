# Alpha Vantage Terms of Service (Alpha Vantage, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** Alpha Vantage licenses its API for personal, non-commercial use unless agreed in writing; the terms say nothing on storing data, derived data or model training, and its economic-indicator and commodity APIs also fall under the FRED API terms.

## Problem
What a research user may do with Alpha Vantage data.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Read the public Terms of Service sections 2-7 and 20.

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- s. 2(a): licence for "personal, non-commercial use" unless agreed otherwise in writing; commercial use includes use beyond "investment analysis, research, testing, monitoring" or on behalf of a firm.
- s. 2(a)(iii): letting others access the information counts as commercial use.
- s. 3: licence non-sublicensable and non-transferable.
- Storage, caching, derived data and ML training: not addressed.
- s. 20: Economic Indicators and Commodities APIs also governed by the FRED API terms (so FRED's AI-training prohibition reaches them: see [[FRED 2026 Legal Terms of Use]]).
- s. 7: on termination access is deactivated; nothing on deleting stored data.

## Limits and risks to validity
Silence is not permission: training rights would need written confirmation (vendor-quote list). Read through a summarising fetch tool.
