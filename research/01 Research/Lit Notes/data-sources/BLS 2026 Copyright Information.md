# BLS copyright information (BLS, 2026)

**Basis:** opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** Everything BLS publishes is public domain (except previously copyrighted images); citation is requested, not required, so BLS data taken from BLS itself can be stored and used for training.

## Problem
Licence of original US statistical releases.

## Data and period
All BLS publications and data.

## Method
Public-domain statement; emblem is a trademark.

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Public domain; BLS asks users to "cite the Bureau of Labor Statistics as the source".

## Limits and risks to validity
Covers BLS only; BEA and Census terms not read this session (assumed similar as US federal works, to confirm). BLS vintages must be reconstructed from release archives.
