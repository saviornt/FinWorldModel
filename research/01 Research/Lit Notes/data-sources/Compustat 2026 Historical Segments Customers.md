# Compustat Historical Segments (customer segments) (S&P Global Market Intelligence, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** Compustat Historical Segments (via WRDS) holds business, geographic and customer segments for North American firms from 1976, including named major customers that firms disclose; academic licences only.

## Problem
A long, filing-based source of customer links.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Aalto University data-hub entry for Compustat (licence, coverage, identifiers).

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Historical Segments from 1976: business, geographic and customer segments.
- Identifiers GVKEY, TIC, CIK, CUSIP.
- Licence: academic and non-commercial research only; WRDS terms apply.

## Limits and risks to validity
Customer links exist only where a customer is at least 10% of revenue (threshold per [[Bacilieri 2023 Firm-level Production Networks]]), and customer names are free text needing matching (used by [[Cohen 2008 Economic Links Predictable Returns]]). Library page, not S&P documentation.
