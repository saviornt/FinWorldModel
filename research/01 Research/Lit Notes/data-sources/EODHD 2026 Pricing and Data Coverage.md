# EODHD pricing and coverage (EODHD, 2026)

**Basis:** opened 2026-11-08: [pricing](https://eodhd.com/pricing) (plans and FAQ); EODHD's [survivorship-bias FAQ](https://eodhd.com/financial-academy/financial-faq/survivorship-bias-free-financial-analysis) seen in search results only (not opened; its `_old` ticker convention is noted as unverified).

**Takeaway:** Lowest price for breadth: $29.99/month for EOD plus intraday worldwide, 100,000 calls/day and 1,000/min; US EOD to 1972, 1-minute US intraday from 2004 (other intervals only from October 2020), ticks from 2008 as a $9.99 add-on; personal-use plans only at these prices.

## Problem
Cost, limits and history of EODHD data.

## Data and period
EOD "30+ yrs" (FAQ: US from the earliest date, Ford from June 1972). Intraday US: "1-minute from 2004, other intervals from October 2020". Tick data from 1 January 2008.

## Method
Free: 20 calls/day, 1 year EOD. Historian $19.99/month (EOD). Active trader $29.99/month (EOD + intraday). Fundamentals $59.99. All-in-one $99.99. Paid: 100,000 calls/day, 1,000/min; intraday requests cost 5 calls. Commercial plans priced separately.
