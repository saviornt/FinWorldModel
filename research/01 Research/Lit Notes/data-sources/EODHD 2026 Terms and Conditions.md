# EODHD Terms and Conditions (EODHD, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** EODHD lets non-professional users store, manipulate and analyse data for private, non-commercial purposes but not redistribute or display it; derived data and ML training are not mentioned.

## Problem
What EODHD's licence allows.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Read the public terms (Personal and Commercial Use of Information sections).

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- s. 1: Non-Professional users may "store, manipulate, and analyze the data" for private non-commercial purposes.
- s. 1: no selling, retransmitting, redistributing, displaying or granting access.
- s. 2: Professional = anyone else, including business use.
- ML training and derived data: not addressed; sales contact for unclear cases.

## Limits and risks to validity
Read through a summarising fetch tool. Silence on training is not permission.
