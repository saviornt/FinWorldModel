# FRED legal: terms of use, API terms and AI restriction (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, 2026)

**Basis:** opened 2026-11-08 (first 100,000 of 128,412 characters; two passes).

**Takeaway:** FRED (which the terms define to include ALFRED) is free for personal use, but its terms forbid using FRED content in developing or training machine-learning or AI systems, and the API terms forbid storing, caching or archiving FRED content; third-party copyrighted series need the owner's permission.

## Problem
What FRED/ALFRED terms allow a forecasting project to do.

## Data and period
All FRED services, including ALFRED.

## Method
Summarised Terms s. II prohibitions apply to "All use of FRED data—including non-commercial, educational, and personal use". These include use "in connection with the development or training of any software program or system or machine learning", naming "large language models, deep learning, generative artificial intelligence". API prohibition (l): "Use the FRED® API in connection with storing, caching, or archiving any portion of the FRED® Services or FRED® Content". Commercial: internal commercial use allowed for series marked public domain or copyrighted with citation; otherwise written consent. Attribution required.

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- A model trained on FRED/ALFRED downloads would breach the terms as read (s. II).
- The original public-domain releases (e.g. BLS, [[BLS 2026 Copyright Information]]) are not bound by FRED's terms; only FRED's copy is.
