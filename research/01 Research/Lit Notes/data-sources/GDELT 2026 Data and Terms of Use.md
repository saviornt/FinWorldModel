# GDELT Project: data downloads and terms of use (GDELT, 2026)

**Basis:** opened 2026-11-08: [data](https://www.gdeltproject.org/data.html) and [about / terms](https://www.gdeltproject.org/about.html). The guide's seed (Aalto DataHub overview) was not opened; GDELT's own pages were used.

**Takeaway:** Free for any academic, commercial or government use, redistributable with citation; GDELT 2.0 events and GKG update every 15 minutes; 1.0 events reach back to 1979 (reduced set to 2014-02-17) and the GKG starts 2013-04-01; everything is also in BigQuery.

## Problem
Coverage, latency and terms of GDELT.

## Data and period
Events: reduced 1.0 set 1979-01-01 to 2014-02-17; GKG 1.0 from 2013-04-01; 2.0 updates every 15 minutes (2.0 start date not on these pages). Monitors news "in more than 100 languages" from "hundreds of thousands" of sources.

## Method
Machine-coded events (CAMEO) and knowledge graph from news; files every 15 minutes; BigQuery tables updated every 15 minutes.

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Licence: "unlimited and unrestricted use for any academic, commercial, or governmental use of any kind without fee" (about).
- Redistribution allowed with a citation and link (about).
- 15-minute update gives a natural knowledge-time stamp per file.

## Limits and risks to validity
Event date versus date-added semantics not described on these pages (RQ3.2). Coding accuracy not assessed here (RP1 RQ1.14 notes cover GDELT signal quality).
