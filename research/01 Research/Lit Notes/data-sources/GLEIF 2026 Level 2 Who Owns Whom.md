# GLEIF Level 2 data: who owns whom, and open data (GLEIF, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** GLEIF's LEI records include each entity's direct and ultimate accounting-consolidating parent (collected since May 2017), free to search and download (golden copy, delta files, API), with website data under CC0.

## Problem
A free, stable entity identifier with parent links.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Level 2 page and Open Data page. Parent relationships follow a March 2016 Regulatory Oversight Committee policy; reporting exceptions allowed (not defined on the page).

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Direct and ultimate accounting-consolidating parents; children shown on the parent's record.
- Collection began May 2017: no ownership history before that.
- Access free without registration: LEI Search, concatenated files, golden copy and delta files, API.
- Open Data page: website data provided under CC0.

## Limits and risks to validity
Coverage limited to entities that hold LEIs (mostly regulated financial-market participants); accounting consolidation is not voting control; whether golden-copy history is point-in-time was not read. LEI Data Terms of Use not opened.
