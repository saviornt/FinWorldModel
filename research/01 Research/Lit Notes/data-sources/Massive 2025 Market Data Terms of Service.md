# Massive Market Data Terms of Service (Massive.com, 2025)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** Massive (formerly Polygon.io) lets individual subscribers download and store data for personal use, but forbids using it to train, fine-tune or distil AI or ML models, requires deletion within 30 days of the licence ending, and lets you keep only your own analyses that cannot recreate the data.

## Problem
What Massive's market data licence allows for storage, derived data and model training.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Read the Market Data Terms (last updated 2025-08-28), which supplement the Individuals and Businesses Terms.

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- s. 1: licence to "access, download, store and use" Information in your plan, personal use.
- s. 5(g): no use of Information "to train, fine-tune or distill" an AI/ML model unless expressly permitted.
- s. 5(c): may keep own analyses not containing Information and not able to recreate it; no redistribution.
- s. 5(d): no index, benchmark or financial product without a separate licence.
- s. 8: delete within 30 days after the licence ends (backups up to 90 days if untouched); CME data must be purged immediately.
- s. 3: Non-Professional means personal, non-business use; misclassification may be back-billed.

## Limits and risks to validity
Vendor terms as read through a summarising fetch tool; Businesses Terms not opened. The training ban blocks the project's core use unless Massive grants written permission (vendor-quote list).
