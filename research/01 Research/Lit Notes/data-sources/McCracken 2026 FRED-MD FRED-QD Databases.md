# FRED-MD and FRED-QD databases page (McCracken, St. Louis Fed, 2026)

**Basis:** opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** Ready-made monthly (FRED-MD) and quarterly (FRED-QD) macro panels for big-data forecasting with archived monthly vintages from 1999-08 (MD) and 2018-05 (QD) to 2026-09, free to download.

## Problem
A large, vintage-dated macro panel ready for forecasting.

## Data and period
FRED-MD vintages 1999-08 to 2014-12 and 2015-01 to 2025-12 (ZIPs), monthly CSVs to 2026-09; FRED-QD 2018-05 onward.

## Method
Series updated through FRED; transformations documented in appendices (not read). Working papers: doi 10.20955/wp.2015.012 (MD), 10.20955/wp.2020.005 (QD).

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Monthly snapshots give a cheap point-in-time macro panel (one vintage per month) without per-series ALFRED calls.

## Limits and risks to validity
Built from FRED; whether FRED's AI-training prohibition ([[FRED 2026 Legal Terms of Use]]) covers these research files is not stated on the page. Vintages are month-end snapshots, not release-time stamps.
