# NOAA Storm Events Database (NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** NOAA's Storm Events Database covers US storms from January 1950 (tornadoes only to 1954; 48 event types from 1996), entered by the National Weather Service, and at access ran to May 2026, a lag of several months.

## Problem
A historical US weather-event source.

Database details page.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Coverage January 1950 – May 2026 (at access on 2026-11-08).
- 1950–54 tornado only; 1955–95 tornado, thunderstorm wind, hail; 1996– 48 event types (NWS Directive 10-1605).
- Procedures changed over time, so records differ by period.

## Limits and risks to validity
Update lag not stated; the roughly 5–6 month lag is inferred from the coverage end date at access (project reading). Changing event definitions break series comparability. Federal data, presumed public domain (terms not read).
