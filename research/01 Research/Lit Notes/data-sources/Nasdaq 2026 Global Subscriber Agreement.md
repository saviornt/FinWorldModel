# NASDAQ OMX Global Subscriber Agreement (Nasdaq, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** The exchange pass-through agreement licenses data to non-professionals for personal use only and to professionals for internal use, bars giving anyone else access, defines the licensed Information to include processed data, and gives Nasdaq information and inspection rights.

## Problem
Which exchange terms flow down to retail and API users of US equity data.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Read the agreement as hosted by Alpaca (copy linked from Alpaca's disclosures page).

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- s. 1a: Non-Professional: "licensed only for personal use".
- s. 1b: Professional: internal business and/or personal use; limited non-continuous excerpts to customers allowed.
- s. 1: may not "sell, lease, furnish or otherwise permit or provide access" to others.
- s. 12: Information includes "any element of Information as used or processed".
- Audit: information requests (non-professional) and premises inspection (professional).
- Storage period and model training not addressed.

## Limits and risks to validity
Hosted copy via a broker; version date not checked. Defining processed data as Information suggests features derived from Nasdaq data stay under the licence (project reading, not legal advice).
