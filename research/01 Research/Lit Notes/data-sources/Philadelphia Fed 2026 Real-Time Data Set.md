# Real-Time Data Set for Macroeconomists (Philadelphia Fed, 2026)

**Basis:** opened 2026-11-08. Terms of use not on the page.

**Takeaway:** The original real-time macro data set: vintage snapshots of major US macro variables with first, second and third releases for selected series, new vintages added monthly, downloadable at no stated cost (and not covered by FRED's terms).

## Problem
Point-in-time macro vintages from a source other than FRED.

## Data and period
Major macro variables; 24 GDI variables recently added with monthly and quarterly vintages. Start date not stated on the page (Croushore's review shows a first vintage of 11/1965 in an illustration: [[Croushore 2011 Frontiers of Real-Time Data Analysis]]).

## Method
Vintage matrices (each column one vintage); release-based files for selected variables.

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Monthly vintages; files rather than an as-of API, so suited to one-time bulk download.

## Limits and risks to validity
Fewer series than ALFRED (headline macro only). Licence terms not stated; assumed public research data, to confirm.
