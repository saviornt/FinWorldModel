# RavenPack News Analytics (Imperial College library guide, 2026)

**Basis:** opened 2026-11-08. A third-party (university library) description, not RavenPack's own page.

**Takeaway:** The standard academic news-analytics feed: Dow Jones Newswires, WSJ and over 19,000 other sources, with millisecond timestamps over 20+ years; access is through institutional subscription.

## Problem
Long-history, precisely timestamped licensed news.

## Data and period
"spanning over 20 years"; millisecond timestamps.

## Method
Entity and event detection with sentiment, relevance and novelty scores.
