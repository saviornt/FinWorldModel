# SEC Form 13F data sets (U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** The SEC publishes quarterly flattened extracts of institutional managers' 13F holdings, as filed, from 2013 Q2 onward, free, with accuracy not guaranteed.

## Problem
Free institutional ownership data.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Data sets page and readme.

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Extracted from the XML part of 13F filings, "without change from the 'as-filed' submissions".
- Quarterly; since March 2024 each set covers the prior three months.
- Files from 2013 Q2 to the June–August 2026 set.
- SEC cannot guarantee accuracy; not a substitute for filings; refreshed 2024-03-12 with processing fixes.

## Limits and risks to validity
Filing lag and reporting thresholds not stated on the page (not confirmed). As-filed data means a filing date is available for knowledge-time (project reading).
