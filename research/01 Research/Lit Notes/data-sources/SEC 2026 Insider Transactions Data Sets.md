# SEC Insider Transactions data sets (Forms 3, 4, 5) (U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** The SEC publishes quarterly as-filed extracts of insider filings (Forms 3, 4, 5) by executives, directors and 10% holders from 2006 Q1, free, accuracy not guaranteed.

## Problem
Free data on insiders, directors and large holders.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Data sets page and documentation.

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- From Section 16 filings by senior executives, directors and 10%+ shareholders.
- Quarterly; coverage 2006 Q1 to September 2026; 7–16 MB per quarter.
- Rule 10b5-1 flag added (2023–2025 sets) since July 2025.
- Accuracy not guaranteed; not a substitute for filings.

## Limits and risks to validity
Covers filers, not full board composition; names need linking to companies and people (entity resolution, RQ3.8).
