# StockTwits Terms of Service (StockTwits, 2026)

**Basis:** terms page opened 2026-11-08 (last revised 2026-07-10). No current official developer pricing page was found; third-party scraper listings were seen in search and not used.

**Takeaway:** Automated extraction is banned except by written permission or an approved API, and StockTwits licenses derived products of user content to financial firms itself; a research corpus needs a licensed feed, not scraping.

## Problem
Can StockTwits messages be collected and used?

## Data and period
Not stated.

## Method
Section 5: "You may not scrape, harvest, mirror, frame, deep-link to, data-mine" except as authorised in writing or via an approved API. Section 8: users grant StockTwits a sublicensable licence; StockTwits may license products derived from public content "to third parties, including financial institutions".

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Scraping is prohibited (s. 5); AI training is not mentioned.
- Historical access, price and API tiers not public on the pages found.

## Limits and risks to validity
Developer docs and data-licensing offers not found; a vendor quote is needed.
