# Twelve Data pricing (Twelve Data, 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** Twelve Data's personal plans run from free (8 credits a minute, 800 a day) to $999 a month, with real-time US stocks from the $79 Grow plan; history depth, intraday resolution, storage and redistribution terms are not stated on the pricing page.

## Problem
Whether Twelve Data is a candidate US equity API.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Read the pricing page (personal plans).

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- Basic $0: 8 API credits/min, 800/day; labelled internal non-display use.
- Grow $79, Pro $229, Ultra $999 a month (17% off yearly); paid plans listed as unlimited daily.
- Commercial pricing on a separate page.
- Credit figures differ between pricing cards and comparison table.

## Limits and risks to validity
Pricing page only; history and licence not read. Internal inconsistency in credit figures.
