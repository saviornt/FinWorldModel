# X Developer Agreement (X Corp., 2026)

**Basis:** official documentation opened 2026-11-08.

**Takeaway:** X's developer agreement forbids using X content to train or fine-tune a foundation or frontier model, requires deleting removed posts within 24 hours of a request and all licensed material at termination, and treats post IDs as X Content.

## Problem
What X allows for storing posts and training models on them.

## Data and period
Not applicable.

## Method
Read the Developer Agreement on docs.x.com; the Developer Policy (incorporated by reference) was not opened.

## Baselines and validation
Not applicable.

## Results (with page or section references)
- s. III(A)(k): no use to "fine-tune or train a foundation or frontier model".
- s. IV(B): delete or modify removed content within 24 hours of a written request.
- s. VII(I): delete all licensed material on termination; evidence on request.
- s. I(12): post IDs are X Content; s. III(A)(d) no redistribution unless permitted.
- s. II(A)(1): analysis only as approved by X.

## Limits and risks to validity
Whether a small task-specific market model is a 'foundation or frontier model' is undefined in the text read (project reading); a narrow model trained on aggregated X sentiment may fall outside the ban, but deletion duties still make a stored corpus hard to keep point-in-time. Developer Policy not read.
