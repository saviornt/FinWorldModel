## 2. Research Principles

Research materials should distinguish between:

- Established findings supported by published evidence.
- Findings with moderate or limited supporting evidence.
- Preliminary observations and hypotheses.
- Original research questions proposed by this Project.
- Experimental results generated by FinWorldModel.

Research conclusions should include appropriate source
references and evidence assessments.

## 3. Reproducibility

Where reasonably possible, experimental documentation
should record:

- Data source and provider.
- Dataset coverage and date ranges.
- Data adjustments and normalization.
- Feature definitions.
- Model configuration and parameters.
- Training and evaluation periods.
- Baseline comparisons.
- Evaluation metrics.
- Known limitations and potential biases.

Research conclusions should be traceable to their
underlying experiments and source material.

## 4. Research Integrity

Original research must be distinguished from previously
published work.

External publications, datasets, and research materials
must retain their original attribution and applicable
licensing restrictions.
