# Research Sources and Bibliography

This directory contains bibliographic references and
source records supporting Financial World Model research.

## Source Record Requirements

Where available, record:

- Publication title.
- Author(s).
- Publication date.
- Journal, conference, or publisher.
- DOI or permanent identifier.
- Official publication URL.
- Publication or access status.
- Relevant research question.
- Evidence strength and limitations.
- Date accessed.

## Source Classification

Research sources may be classified as:

- Strong evidence.
- Moderate evidence.
- Weak evidence.
- Preliminary or unverified evidence.

Classification should be based on research quality,
methodology, reproducibility, and relevance.

## Copyright and Redistribution

Bibliographic references and links should be preferred
over redistribution of complete third-party publications.

Publisher PDFs, copyrighted articles, datasets, and
other externally owned materials must not be redistributed
unless the applicable rights permit it.

Original summaries and analyses should clearly distinguish
the Project author's work from the original publication.

## Research Integrity

Sources must not be fabricated, misrepresented, or
cited as supporting conclusions they do not establish.
